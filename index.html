About

Connecting Westchester Volunteers with Community Need

Care & Connect is a free resource that makes it easy to find volunteer opportunities across Westchester County. We've gathered local nonprofits, their programs, and age requirements in one place — so you can spend less time searching and more time giving back.

Whether you're a student looking for service hours, a family wanting to pitch in together, or an adult ready to commit to a cause, there's a place for you here.
